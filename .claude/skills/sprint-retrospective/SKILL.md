# Sprint Retrospective

## Retrospective Formats

Choose the format that fits the team's mood and available time.

### Start / Stop / Continue (15-20 min)
Simple and direct:
- **Start:** What should we begin doing?
- **Stop:** What should we stop doing?
- **Continue:** What's working well that we should keep?

### 4Ls (20-30 min)
More nuanced reflection:
- **Liked:** What did you enjoy?
- **Learned:** What did you learn?
- **Lacked:** What was missing?
- **Longed for:** What do you wish you had?

### Mad / Sad / Glad (15-20 min)
Emotion-centered:
- **Mad:** What frustrated you?
- **Sad:** What disappointed you?
- **Glad:** What made you happy?

## Process

### 1. Gather Input
- Review `docs/sprint-journal/` for a timeline of activities
- Review `docs/design-decisions/` for choices made
- Check `docs/test-results/` for outcomes (if Test phase completed)

### 2. Individual Reflection (5 min)
Each team member writes their thoughts privately using the chosen format.

### 3. Share & Cluster (10 min)
- Each person shares their points
- Group similar items together
- Identify themes

### 4. Vote on Priorities (3 min)
Each person gets 2-3 votes on which themes matter most.

### 5. Action Items (5-10 min)
For the top 2-3 themes, define:
- **What** specifically to change
- **Who** is responsible
- **When** by

## Output

Save to `docs/sprint-journal/retrospective.md` using the template at `assets/templates/retro-template.md`.

## When to Run

- **End of sprint:** Full retrospective on the entire sprint process
- **Mid-sprint:** Quick retro if the team feels stuck or wants to adjust process
- **After testing:** Reflect specifically on what testing revealed about the sprint's assumptions

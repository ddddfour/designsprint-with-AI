# Usability Test Script

**Prototype:** [Description of what's being tested]
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Moderator:** [Name]

## Welcome (3 min)

"Thank you for taking the time to help us today. We're testing a prototype of [product description]. We're testing the design, not you — there are no wrong answers. Please think aloud as you use the prototype, telling us what you're looking at, thinking, and trying to do. Do you have any questions before we start?"

## Background Questions (5 min)

1. Can you tell me a bit about yourself and how you currently [relevant activity]?
2. What tools or apps do you currently use for [relevant task]?
3. What's the most frustrating part of [relevant experience]?

**Scenario:** "[Context-setting scenario]"

**Success criteria:**
- [ ] ...

**Observe:** [What specifically to watch for]

### Task 3: [Exploratory task]
**Scenario:** "[Open-ended scenario]"

**Observe:** [What specifically to watch for]

## Follow-up Questions (5-7 min)

1. What was the easiest part of what you just did?
2. What was the most confusing or frustrating part?
3. If you could change one thing about this experience, what would it be?
4. On a scale of 1-5, how likely would you be to use something like this? Why?
5. [Sprint-question-specific question]

## Wrap-up (2 min)

"Thank you for your feedback — it's very helpful. Is there anything else you'd like to share about the experience?"
